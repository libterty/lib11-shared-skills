💡 Best Practices & Tips

1
Privacy First: Always anonymize data and get consent before analyzing team communications. Focus on themes, not individuals.
2
Context Matters: Provide the AI with context about recent events (launches, reorganizations, etc.) that might influence sentiment.
3
Regular Analysis: Run sentiment analysis monthly or quarterly to track trends and identify patterns over time.
4
Action-Oriented: Always ask the AI to provide specific, actionable recommendations based on the sentiment findings.
5
Validate Insights: Cross-reference AI analysis with your own observations and follow up with 1:1 conversations when needed.
6
Share Transparently: Consider sharing sentiment trends (not individual responses) with the team to show you're listening.
