🎯 AI Coaching Suggestion Prompt

Engineering Management Tool for Direct Report Growth Planning

📋 What is this tool?

This template helps engineering managers create comprehensive AI prompts to generate personalized growth and development suggestions for their direct reports. By providing structured context about an engineer's current situation, performance, and career goals, you can leverage AI to identify specific coaching opportunities, skill development areas, and career advancement strategies.

Why use this: Saves time in coaching preparation, ensures comprehensive coverage of growth areas, provides objective suggestions, and helps identify blind spots in development planning.

🤖 Generated AI Prompt
You are an experienced engineering manager and career coach. Based on the following information about one of my direct reports, please provide specific, actionable coaching suggestions and growth opportunities. **Employee Profile:** Name: [Employee Name] Role: [Current Role & Level] Tenure: [Time in Current Role] Team Context: [Team & Tech Stack] **Current Performance:** Strengths & Achievements: [Key Strengths & Recent Achievements] Performance Level: [Current Performance Level] **Growth & Development:** Areas for Development: [Areas for Development] Current Challenges: [Current Challenges or Blockers] **Career Aspirations:** Goals (6-18 months): [Career Goals] Interests & Preferences: [Professional Interests & Preferences] **Focus Areas:** [Selected Focus Areas] Please provide: 1. **Top 3 Growth Opportunities** - Specific areas where this person can develop, with concrete examples 2. **Actionable Development Plan** - 3-5 specific actions they can take in the next 3 months 3. **Skill-Building Resources** - Books, courses, projects, or experiences that align with their goals 4. **Stretch Assignments** - Project ideas or responsibilities that would challenge them appropriately 5. **Potential Blind Spots** - Areas they might not be aware they need to develop 6. **Coaching Conversation Starters** - 3-4 thoughtful questions to use in your next 1:1 Tailor all suggestions to their current level, team context, and career aspirations. Be specific and actionable rather than generic.

💡 Example Usage

Scenario: Mid-level engineer looking to become a tech lead

Input: Strong technical skills but needs to develop system design thinking and cross-team communication

AI Output: Specific recommendations like "Lead the next architecture review session," "Take the 'Designing Data-Intensive Applications' course," and "Shadow the current tech lead during stakeholder meetings"

💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

  • Be Specific: Include concrete examples of strengths and challenges rather than generic statements
  • Context Matters: Mention team dynamics, current projects, and company culture when relevant
  • Update Regularly: Refresh the prompt quarterly or after major projects/changes
  • Follow Up: Use the AI suggestions as a starting point for deeper 1:1 conversations
  • Personalize: Adapt the suggested focus areas based on what you know motivates this person
