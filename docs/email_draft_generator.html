📚 Email Templates

Sprint Status Update
Use Case: Weekly/bi-weekly sprint progress communication
Subject: Sprint [X] Update - [Project Name] Hi [Team/Stakeholders], Here's our Sprint [X] status for [Project]: 📊 PROGRESS • Completed: [X/Y] story points • On track: [Feature list] • At risk: [Issue list] 🎯 NEXT SPRINT • Focus: [Primary objectives] • Delivery: [Expected outcomes] 🚨 BLOCKERS & RISKS • [Issue]: [Impact & resolution plan] Let me know if you have questions. Best, [Name]
Incident Communication
Use Case: Outage or critical issue notification
Subject: [RESOLVED/ONGOING] - [System] Issue Hi [Stakeholders], STATUS: [Resolved/Investigating] IMPACT: [User/system impact] TIMEFRAME: [Start - End time] SUMMARY: [Brief description of issue] RESOLUTION: [Actions taken/being taken] NEXT STEPS: • Post-mortem: [Date] • Prevention: [Measures] I'll provide updates as available. [Name]
Executive Summary
Use Case: High-level leadership updates
Subject: Engineering Update - [Period] [Executive Team], KEY HIGHLIGHTS: ✅ [Major achievement] ✅ [Milestone reached] 📈 [Metric improvement] PRIORITIES & PROGRESS: 1. [Initiative]: [Status & impact] 2. [Initiative]: [Status & impact] RISKS & MITIGATION: • [Risk]: [Action plan] RESOURCE NEEDS: • [Request]: [Justification] Happy to discuss details. [Name]
Team Announcement
Use Case: Process changes, new hires, policy updates
Subject: [Team] Update - [Topic] Team, I wanted to share some important updates: 📢 ANNOUNCEMENT: [Main news/change] 💡 WHY THIS MATTERS: [Context and benefits] 🔄 WHAT'S CHANGING: • [Change 1] • [Change 2] ⏰ TIMELINE: • [Date]: [Milestone] • [Date]: [Implementation] QUESTIONS? Feel free to reach out or we'll discuss in our next team meeting. Thanks, [Name]
