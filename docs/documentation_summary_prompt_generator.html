❓ Knowledge Extraction Prompts

Select specific questions and prompts to include in your knowledge extraction framework:

Process & Procedure Questions
Walk me through each step of this process, including what happens between the obvious steps.
What needs to be in place before someone can start this process?
How do you know when each step has been completed successfully?
Are there different ways to do this depending on the situation? What determines which approach to use?
Problem-Solving & Troubleshooting
What are the most common problems you encounter with this system/process?
When something goes wrong, what's your diagnostic process? What do you check first?
What are the early warning signs that indicate a problem is developing?
If everything breaks, what's the recovery process? What's the fastest way to get back to working state?
Context & Decision History
Why was this system/process designed this way? What problem was it solving?
What other approaches were considered? Why weren't they chosen?
How has this evolved over time? What lessons have been learned?
What constraints or limitations should people be aware of? What can't/shouldn't be changed?
Knowledge Transfer & Learning
What's the best way for someone new to learn this? What order should they learn things in?
What mistakes do people commonly make when learning this? How can we prevent them?
What separates a beginner from an expert in this area? What intuition do experts have?
What resources, tools, or references are essential for working with this effectively?
