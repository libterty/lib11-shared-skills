Use this template to set clear expectations, track progress, and provide consistent onboarding experiences. Each phase builds upon the previous one to gradually increase responsibility and independence.

Phase 1: First 30 Days
Foundation & Setup
0% Complete

Phase 1 Goals

Focus on orientation, basic setup, and getting familiar with the team, processes, and codebase. By the end of 30 days, the new hire should feel welcomed, have all necessary access, and understand the team dynamics.

Week 1: Orientation & Setup
  • Complete HR onboarding paperwork and orientation
  • Set up development environment (IDE, tools, accounts)
  • Obtain necessary system access and permissions
  • Meet with direct manager for role expectations discussion
  • Meet assigned buddy/mentor for team introduction
  • Attend team meetings as observer
  • Review team documentation and coding standards
Week 2-3: Learning & Exploration
  • Complete codebase walkthrough with senior team member
  • Set up local development environment and run tests
  • Shadow team members during code reviews
  • Complete assigned learning modules/documentation
  • Understand deployment process and CI/CD pipeline
  • Begin first small task (bug fix or documentation update)
Week 4: First Contributions
  • Submit first pull request with mentor review
  • Participate actively in team meetings and standups
  • Complete 30-day check-in meeting with manager
  • Provide feedback on onboarding process

Phase 2: Days 31-60
Building Competence
0% Complete

Phase 2 Goals

Increase independence and responsibility. Take on meaningful work with guidance. Start contributing to team discussions and begin developing expertise in specific areas.

Technical Growth
  • Take ownership of a small feature or improvement
  • Conduct independent code reviews for simple changes
  • Participate in architecture/design discussions
  • Complete advanced training in key technologies
  • Understand monitoring and debugging tools
Team Integration
  • Lead or present in a team meeting
  • Collaborate with other teams (product, design, etc.)
  • Mentor/help newer team members or interns
  • Contribute to team process improvements
  • Attend relevant company all-hands or engineering meetings
Skills Development
  • Complete incident response training
  • Understand team's on-call procedures
  • Shadow senior engineers during on-call rotation
  • Complete 60-day performance check-in

60-Day Notes & Observations

Phase 3: Days 61-90
Full Integration & Independence
0% Complete

Phase 3 Goals

Operate independently as a full team member. Take ownership of significant work, contribute to team strategy, and be ready for standard performance evaluation cycles.

Full Ownership
  • Own a significant project or feature end-to-end
  • Participate in planning and estimation meetings
  • Make architectural decisions for owned components
  • Handle production issues independently
  • Join on-call rotation (if applicable)
Leadership & Growth
  • Lead a technical discussion or design review
  • Mentor new team members or interns
  • Contribute to hiring process (interviews, technical screens)
  • Identify and propose process improvements
  • Set personal development goals for next quarter
90-Day Evaluation
  • Complete formal 90-day performance review
  • Receive 360-degree feedback from team members
  • Discuss career development path with manager
  • Set goals for next performance cycle
  • Complete onboarding feedback survey
