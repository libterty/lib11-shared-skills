💡 Tips for Effective Async Updates

  • 1
    Be Specific: Use concrete details, numbers, and outcomes rather than vague statements like "made progress."
  • 2
    Context Matters: Explain the "why" behind your work and how it connects to team/company goals.
  • 3
    Call for Help: Be explicit about what kind of help you need and who can provide it.
  • 4
    Consistent Timing: Share updates at the same time each day/week so teammates know when to expect them.
  • 5
    Use @mentions: Tag relevant team members to ensure important updates reach the right people.
  • 6
    Link Resources: Include links to PRs, tickets, documents, or demos to provide more context.
