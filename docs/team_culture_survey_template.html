Team Culture & Feedback Survey

Purpose: This survey helps engineering managers assess team health, culture, and gather actionable feedback to improve team effectiveness. Use this template quarterly or after major changes to track team sentiment and identify areas for improvement. All responses can be anonymous to encourage honest feedback.
📌 Note: This survey can be conducted anonymously. Consider using tools like Google Forms, Typeform, or internal survey platforms to collect responses privately and aggregate results.
