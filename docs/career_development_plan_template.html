📈 Career Growth & Development Plan

Structured Professional Development Planning Tool

📋 What is this tool?

This comprehensive template helps engineering managers create detailed, actionable career development plans for their direct reports. It provides a structured approach to setting goals, tracking progress, identifying skill gaps, and planning growth activities that align with both individual aspirations and business needs.

Why use this: Creates clear development roadmaps, tracks progress objectively, ensures alignment between personal and company goals, facilitates meaningful career conversations, and provides documentation for performance reviews and promotions.
