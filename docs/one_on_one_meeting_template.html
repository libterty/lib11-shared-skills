💡 Quick Check-in Questions (Choose 1-2)

Select questions to spark deeper conversations when needed:

  • What's energizing you most about your work right now?
  • What's been your biggest challenge this week?
  • Is there anything I should be doing differently as your manager?
  • What would make your job easier or more enjoyable?
  • How connected do you feel to the team's mission and goals?
  • What's one thing you'd like to improve about our team?
  • Is there anyone on the team you'd like to work with more?
  • What's been the highlight of your month so far?
