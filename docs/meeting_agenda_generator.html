    🎯 Sprint Planning Meeting
    Duration: 2 hours | Attendees: Dev Team + PO
    🎯 SPRINT 12 PLANNING ⏱️ Total Time: 120 minutes ⏰ AGENDA: 1. Sprint Goal Definition (15 min) • Review product priorities • Define sprint success criteria 2. Backlog Review (30 min) • Story walkthrough • Acceptance criteria clarification • Dependencies identification 3. Capacity Planning (20 min) • Team availability review • Velocity consideration • Commitment discussion 4. Story Estimation (45 min) • Planning poker sessions • Risk assessment • Task breakdown 5. Sprint Commitment (10 min) • Final story selection • Sprint goal confirmation 📋 PREPARATION NEEDED: • Stories refined & prioritized • Team capacity calculated • Previous sprint metrics ready
    🤝 Engineering 1:1 Meeting
    Duration: 45 minutes | Frequency: Bi-weekly
    🤝 1:1: [ENGINEER NAME] ⏱️ Duration: 45 minutes ⏰ AGENDA: 1. Check-in & Wellbeing (5 min) • How are you feeling overall? • Work-life balance status 2. Current Work Discussion (15 min) • Project progress & challenges • Blockers or support needed • Technical decisions/questions 3. Career Development (15 min) • Goal progress review • Skill development opportunities • Growth areas to focus on 4. Team & Process Feedback (5 min) • Team dynamics observations • Process improvement ideas 5. Future Planning (5 min) • Upcoming assignments • Next quarter interests 📌 FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS: • Document key decisions • Update career development plan • Schedule follow-up meetings if needed
    🚨 Incident Postmortem
    Duration: 90 minutes | Focus: Blameless analysis
    🚨 INCIDENT POSTMORTEM Incident: [NAME] | Date: [DATE] ⏱️ Duration: 90 minutes ⏰ AGENDA: 1. Incident Summary (10 min) • Timeline overview • Impact assessment • Key metrics affected 2. Detailed Timeline Review (25 min) • Chronological event walkthrough • Decision points analysis • Communication review 3. Root Cause Analysis (25 min) • Contributing factors identification • System weakness analysis • Process gap review 4. Action Items Development (20 min) • Immediate fixes (< 1 week) • Medium-term improvements (< 1 month) • Long-term preventions (< 3 months) 5. Process Improvements (10 min) • Monitoring enhancements • Alert optimization • Documentation updates 🎯 SUCCESS CRITERIA: • Clear action items with owners • Prevention strategy defined • Lessons learned documented
    🏛️ Architecture Review
    Duration: 90 minutes | Focus: Technical decisions
    🏛️ ARCHITECTURE REVIEW System: [NAME] | Reviewer: [ARCHITECT] ⏱️ Duration: 90 minutes ⏰ AGENDA: 1. Problem Statement (10 min) • Business requirements review • Technical constraints • Success criteria definition 2. Proposed Solution Overview (20 min) • High-level architecture • Technology choices rationale • Integration points 3. Deep Dive Discussion (40 min) • Scalability considerations • Security implications • Performance characteristics • Maintainability aspects 4. Alternative Approaches (10 min) • Options considered • Trade-offs analysis • Decision rationale 5. Risk Assessment (10 min) • Technical risks • Implementation challenges • Mitigation strategies 📦 DELIVERABLES: • Architecture decision record (ADR) • Implementation recommendations • Risk mitigation plan
    📊 Weekly Upward Report
    Duration: 20-30 minutes | Attendees: You + Leadership
    📊 WEEKLY UPWARD STATUS REPORT [Team/Project Name] ⏱️ Duration: 30 minutes ⏰ AGENDA: 1. Headline Status (5 min) • Overall status: on track / at risk / off track • Top 2-3 wins this week • Top 2-3 risks 2. Delivery vs. Goals (8 min) • Progress against committed milestones/quarterly goals • What changed since last update 3. Decisions Needed (8 min) • Decision 1: options, trade-offs, your recommendation • Decision 2 (if any): same format 4. Risks & Escalations (4 min) • What needs leadership awareness or support right now • Trigger conditions for each risk 5. Next Week Focus (5 min) • Key priorities for next week • What you'll report on next time 🎯 SUCCESS CRITERIA: • Leadership leaves with the 2-3 things they need to know or decide • No status buried — risks and decisions surfaced, not just progress
    ⏱️ Meeting Time Optimization Tips

    15 min: Status checks, quick decisions | 30 min: Deep dives, problem-solving | 60 min: Planning, reviews | 90+ min: Workshops, comprehensive planning
