🔒 Privacy Reminder

Before pasting this prompt into a third-party AI tool, review the details you've entered. Avoid including information that is confidential, sensitive, or that the team member wouldn't expect to be shared outside a private conversation — e.g. medical, legal, or compensation details. When in doubt, generalize or omit rather than paste as-is.

📋 Example AI-Generated 1:1 Agenda

Once you paste the generated prompt into an AI tool, expect an output roughly like:

  • 5-7 prioritized discussion points tailored to what you entered
  • A short rationale for why each point matters right now
  • Suggested open-ended questions for each point
  • A recommended closing question to check in on support needed
💡 Best Practices for AI-Generated 1:1 Prep
  • Treat the AI's output as a draft agenda, not a script — adapt it to how the conversation actually goes
  • Cross-check any suggested talking point against what you actually know to be true
  • Don't let the AI's phrasing replace your own judgment on sensitive topics (performance, conflict, personal matters)
  • Update the historical context fields after each 1:1 so future prompts stay accurate
