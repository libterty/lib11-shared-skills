💡 Tips for Effective Team Update Emails

1
Keep it Scannable: Use bullet points, clear headers, and concise language. Busy stakeholders should get key info quickly.
2
Lead with Impact: Start with outcomes and business value, not just activities. Show how work connects to company goals.
3
Be Transparent about Challenges: Don't hide problems. Share challenges early with context and proposed solutions.
4
Include Relevant Metrics: Choose 3-5 metrics that matter to your audience. Provide context for trends and changes.
5
Call Out Dependencies: Highlight where you need decisions, resources, or support from other teams or leadership.
6
Maintain Consistent Timing: Send updates on the same day/time each week so stakeholders know when to expect them.
