Purpose & Usage

This template helps engineering managers prepare for and respond to crisis situations through structured scenario planning, clear escalation procedures, and coordinated response protocols. Use this during crisis preparation, active incident response, and post-crisis analysis to ensure systematic handling of critical situations affecting engineering systems, teams, or business operations.

⚡ Immediate Response Checklist (First 30 Minutes)
  • Acknowledge incident and assign Incident Commander
  • Create incident channel/war room (Slack, Teams, etc.)
  • Notify key stakeholders and on-call team
  • Begin impact assessment and customer communication
  • Start incident timeline documentation
  • Implement immediate containment measures
  • Activate monitoring and logging
  • Schedule regular update cadence (every 15-30 min)
  • Prepare status page update (if applicable)
  • Consider escalation to executive team (P0/P1 incidents)

📋 Post-Crisis Actions
  • Document complete incident timeline
  • Conduct blameless postmortem within 48 hours
  • Identify root causes and contributing factors
  • Create action items with owners and due dates
  • Update runbooks and documentation
  • Review and update crisis response procedures
  • Share lessons learned with broader team
  • Follow up on customer communications
  • Update monitoring and alerting systems
  • Schedule review of action item completion
