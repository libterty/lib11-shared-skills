Engineering Manager Personal 30/60/90-Day Action Plan

Purpose: This template helps engineering managers create a structured personal development plan focused on leadership growth, technical skills, and team impact. Unlike new hire onboarding plans, this focuses on continuous professional development and strategic career advancement.

How to Use: Set specific, measurable goals for each time period. Focus on building skills, relationships, and delivering measurable impact. Review and adjust regularly based on progress and changing priorities.
