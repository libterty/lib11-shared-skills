🎯 What is this tool?

This template helps engineering managers systematically analyze skill gaps across their team using AI. By documenting each engineer's current skill levels against required competencies, you can generate comprehensive reports that identify missing skills, prioritize development areas, and create targeted learning plans.

Why use this: Provides objective skill assessment, identifies team-wide capability gaps, enables data-driven hiring decisions, creates personalized development roadmaps, and ensures strategic alignment between team skills and business needs.

💡 Pro Tips for Effective Analysis

  • Be Honest: Accurate skill ratings lead to better recommendations - avoid inflating levels
  • Include Context: Mention specific technologies, frameworks, and business requirements
  • Update Regularly: Refresh skill assessments quarterly or after major project completions
  • Focus on Outcomes: Link skill gaps to specific business impacts or project delays
  • Consider Soft Skills: Don't forget leadership, communication, and mentoring capabilities
  • Plan for Growth: Include aspirational skills needed for future projects and roles
