🎯 What is this tool?

This template helps engineering managers create comprehensive AI prompts that generate personalized career path recommendations, promotion roadmaps, and skill development plans for their team members. Why use this: Provides data-driven career guidance, identifies skill gaps and development opportunities, creates clear promotion pathways, suggests relevant learning resources, and helps align individual goals with business needs and industry trends.

💡 Tips for Effective Career Path Guidance

  • Be Specific: Include concrete examples of current skills and achievements for accurate assessment
  • Consider Multiple Paths: Don't limit to one career track - explore hybrid and emerging roles
  • Timeline Reality: Set realistic expectations based on current level and company promotion cycles
  • Skills vs. Experience: Balance learning new skills with gaining experience applying existing ones
  • Company Alignment: Consider how career goals align with company needs and opportunities
  • Regular Updates: Revisit and update career plans quarterly as skills and interests evolve
