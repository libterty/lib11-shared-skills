📝 Documentation Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to ensure comprehensive documentation for each knowledge area:

Clear Purpose: Document explains why this process/system exists and its business value
Step-by-Step Instructions: Detailed procedures that someone unfamiliar can follow
Prerequisites & Dependencies: What needs to be in place before starting
Examples & Screenshots: Visual aids and real examples to clarify complex steps
Troubleshooting Guide: Common issues and their solutions
Success Criteria: How to verify the process completed correctly
Historical Context: Why decisions were made, what was tried before
Owner & Contacts: Who maintains this, who to ask questions
Review Schedule: When and how often this documentation should be updated
